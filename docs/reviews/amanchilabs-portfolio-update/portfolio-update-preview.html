Your business finances. Organized. Understood. Tax-ready.

BUSINESS BOOKKEEPING & TAX INTELLIGENCE

A comprehensive bookkeeping and tax-readiness application designed for LLC owners who want greater control over their financial records.

Track business transactions, manage member ownership and contributions, understand financial performance, and prepare organized information for tax reporting.

Core capabilities

Business Bookkeeping

Income, expenses, transaction categorization, and financial records.

Member Ownership & Capital

Track member ownership percentages, contributions, distributions, and capital accounts.

Financial Reporting

Profit and loss, financial summaries, and exportable business records.

Tax Readiness

Designed to support Schedule C and multi-member LLC partnership reporting workflows, including Form 1065 and Schedule K-1 preparation.

Ownership & Allocation Tracking

Planned support for ownership changes, partner allocations, tax basis, and at-risk calculations.

Privacy-First Architecture

Designed to keep business financial information under the owner's control, with local-first processing and optional intelligence features.

Built for business owners who want clarity and control. Get in touch about LLCBookKeepingTaxReturn
