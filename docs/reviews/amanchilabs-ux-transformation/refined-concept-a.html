Active View · Why It Matters

Your life is not a dataset.

Your financial transactions, skincare notes, and career transcripts are intimate parts of your everyday life. Using modern software shouldn't automatically turn them into another corporation's training set or stored cloud asset.

Sovereign Data Storage

Local storage ensures your personal records remain on physical hardware you own. If your connection drops, your software keeps functioning.

Conscious AI Boundaries

Convenience should come with understandable boundaries. You authorize what information leaves your device, and external provider policies apply.

No Subscription Hostage

Your records live in standard open SQLite formats under your control, free from predatory vendor lock-in or forced account migration.

Eliminating Centralized Breaches

Data that is never centralized cannot be breached in bulk cloud database leaks. Local confinement minimizes unnecessary surface exposure.
